Verified findings

Three deliveries per timestamp are three users, not proof of duplicate feed deliveries. HTTP success and backend selection do not establish database counts or complete historical coverage.
2. Persistence: production Postgres has an actual volume at /var/lib/postgresql/data; production app also has /data. Legacy SQLite file was reported last modified September 2 and is not the active store. A recoverable PostgreSQL backup has not been verified.
3. Shadow collection blocker: /internal/shadow/tradingview validates and acknowledges mirrored packets, but does not call analysis or insert market snapshots/thesis events. Existing schema and successful pre-deploy connectivity checks do not establish learning collection. Other event-writing paths exist but are not reached by this receiver.
4. Shadow persistence blocker: shadow Postgres currently has no attached persistent volume. Current row counts and recoverable backup status must be established before any restart, redeployment or volume change.
5. Outcome accounting: production advances open validation trades for each incoming packet without checking symbol or market timestamp. Candle storage upserts by user/time, but that does not deduplicate trade advancement. Older or repeated bars can therefore alter outcomes or bars_open. Data gaps do not explicitly mark uncertain outcomes.
6. Same-bar ambiguity: STOP is conservatively prioritized when stop and TP2 both fall within a bar, but tp1_hit is still recorded. That flag cannot prove TP1 was reached before the stop; do not present it as confirmed TP1-first success.

Offline verification
